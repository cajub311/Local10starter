👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 MN Paid Family & Medical Leave (PFML) — 2026

New baby, caring for elderly parents, your own medical or mental health — PFML covers it all.

  • ✅ What it covers: Family leave (new child, caring for seriously ill family member) AND medical leave (your own serious health condition, including mental health), plus military leave
  • ✅ Eligibility: Must have earned at least $3,500 in MN wages in the base period
  • ✅ Pay: Partial wage replacement — ~90% for lower earners, ~66% above state average wage
  • ✅ Job protection: Your employer must hold your position — you return to the same or equivalent job
  • ✅ How to apply: Through the MN PFML program at paidleave.mn.gov
  • ✅ Effective Jan 1, 2026 — you're covered if you work in MN
  • ⚠️ PFML is a state program — ask your Steward how it interacts with your union-negotiated leave benefits
