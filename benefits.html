👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 MN Paid Family & Medical Leave (PFML) — 2026

Paid Leave is Minnesota's state program for your own serious health condition or family caregiving needs.

  • ✅ Starts: Jan. 1, 2026
  • ✅ Length: Up to 12 weeks medical leave, up to 12 weeks family leave, up to 20 weeks total in one benefit year
  • ✅ Pay: Partial wage replacement based on income (state formula)
  • ✅ Job protection: Return to same or equivalent position when leave ends
  • ✅ How to apply: Apply at paidleave.mn.gov as soon as you know your leave dates
  • ✅ PFML vs short-term disability: STD usually covers only your own condition; PFML also covers family leave. Ask HR how benefits coordinate so you avoid delays or overpayments.
  • ⚠️ If HR denies or delays leave/pay, contact your steward immediately.
