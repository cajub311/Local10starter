🍼 MN Paid Family & Medical Leave (PFML) — 2026

  • ✅ Up to 12 weeks paid leave per benefit year
  • ✅ Effective Jan 1, 2026 — you're already covered if you work in MN
  • ✅ Covers: welcoming a new child, serious personal illness, caring for a seriously ill family member, military leave
  • ✅ Pay rate: ~90% of wages up to the state average wage, then ~66% above that
  • ✅ Job protected: you return to the same or equivalent position
  • ✅ Apply at: paidleave.mn.gov
  • ⚠️ PFML is a state program — ask your Steward how it interacts with your union-negotiated leave benefits

⏱️ Meal & Rest Breaks — 2026

Expanded under 2026 MN law: 30-minute meal break and 10-minute paid rest break per shift. See contract Section 5B. If these are not being provided, contact your Steward — this is a contractual and legal right.
