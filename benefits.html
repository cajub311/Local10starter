📋 PREVAILING WAGE

On public works projects, you must be paid the prevailing wage for your classification.

  • Rates vary by county and classification (journeyman, apprentice, etc.)
  • Check your contract for current rates — or call the hall
  • MN Dept of Labor: dli.mn.gov/workers/prevailing-wage

👶 PFML — PAID FAMILY & MEDICAL LEAVE

MN PFML started January 1, 2026.

  • Up to 12 weeks paid leave per year
  • For birth/adoption, serious health condition, family care
  • Partial wage replacement (up to state max)
  • Apply at mn.gov/pfml

Your contract may have additional leave. Check with your steward.

💵 MINIMUM WAGE — MN 2026

Minnesota minimum wage (2026):

  • Statewide: $11.41/hr (large employers)
  • Minneapolis / St. Paul: $16.37/hr (local ordinances)

Union rates are typically well above minimum. If you're paid less than contract, file a grievance.

🏥 HEALTH & PENSION

Your contract specifies health, pension, and other benefits.

  • Health insurance — Employer contributions per contract
  • Pension — SMART pension plan; vesting rules apply
  • Vacation / sick — See contract §5B for meal/rest and PTO

For specific plan details, call the hall or check your contract.
