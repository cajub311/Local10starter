Prevailing Wages

What is it? The minimum pay rate the state requires for workers on public construction projects — schools, government buildings, roads, etc. It's set by MN DLI and is usually higher than private-sector rates.

If you work on a state/federal project, you're entitled to these rates (e.g., ~$56/hr sheet metal + fringes). Check DLI for current rates by county.
