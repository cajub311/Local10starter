Weingarten Rights

Read this OUT LOUD to management before answering ANY questions:

“If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, or affect my personal working conditions, I respectfully request that my union representative be present at this meeting. Without representation, I choose not to answer any questions.”
  • You may remain silent until your steward arrives.
  • Your rep can speak, advise you, and take notes.
  • Management CANNOT legally discipline you for invoking this right.
  • If they deny your request, they commit an Unfair Labor Practice (ULP).

🚧 Safety

Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

MN Statute § 182.654, Subd. 11 An employee may refuse to work under conditions that the employee reasonably believes pose an imminent danger of death or serious physical harm. The employer may not retaliate against the employee for exercising this right.
“I am exercising my right under Minnesota Statute 182.654 to refuse this work assignment because I believe it presents an imminent danger of serious injury. I am requesting a safety inspection and will remain available for other safe duties.”
  • Document the hazard — take photos/video if safe to do so.
  • Notify your supervisor AND your steward immediately.
  • Stay on site and available for reassignment.
  • File an OSHA complaint: 1-800-321-6742

💰 Wage Theft

Wage Theft — Protect Your Pay

Minnesota law requires employers to pay all earned wages on time. If your pay is short, late, or missing, take action immediately.

“I believe my wages are not correct. Under MN Statute § 181.101, I am entitled to receive all earned wages on the scheduled payday. I am documenting this discrepancy and requesting an immediate review of my pay records.”
  • Save every pay stub — photograph them now.
  • Track your own hours in a notebook or phone app.
  • Report to MN Dept. of Labor & Industry: 651-284-5005
  • Your union will file a grievance on your behalf.
