🤝 Know Your Rights — In Your Pocket
🛡️
Weingarten Rights — Read If Boss Asks Questions

Before you answer any questions from a supervisor that could lead to discipline, say this word-for-word:

"If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, I respectfully request that my union representative be present at this meeting. Without representation, I choose not to answer any questions."

🦺 Shop Floor Safety Toolkit

If something feels unsafe — stop work, document, call your steward. You have the legal right to refuse work that's unsafe. Period.

🚨 911 Emergency 📞 Business Rep — Joel ✋ Refuse Unsafe Work ⚠️ Report Near-Miss
✅ Pre-Shift 60-Second Checklist
  • Hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toes, hi-vis — on and intact?
  • Know where the first-aid kit, eye-wash, AED, and fire extinguisher are?
  • Emergency exits clear? Muster point known?
  • Water and cooling area available (heat days)?
  • Fall protection: anchor point rated 5,000 lbs? Lanyard/harness inspected?
  • Lock-out/tag-out: your lock on? Verified zero energy?
  • Tools inspected — no cracked handles, frayed cords, missing guards?
  • Ladder: 4-to-1 ratio, 3-ft extension above landing, feet locked?
🚑 If Someone's Hurt — Do This
  1. Call 911 first if serious. Don't "tough it out" on a head hit or fall.
  2. Notify your foreman and your steward — same day.
  3. Get seen at urgent care or the ER. Ask for a copy of the report.
  4. File a written incident report with the company within 24 hours.
  5. Workers' comp claim — you have the right to file. Do not sign anything before calling the hall.
  6. Take photos of the scene, equipment, and any visible injuries.
  7. Write down witness names before the shift ends.
🛡️ Your OSHA Rights (Quick Card)
  • Right to Know: SDS/chemical info, workplace hazards.
  • Right to Refuse: imminent danger work — you cannot be fired for it.
  • Right to File: OSHA complaint confidentially. 1-800-321-OSHA (6742).
  • Right to Training: in a language you understand.
  • Right to See Records: injury logs, exposure data, medical records.
  • Right to Rep: steward present in any safety investigation.

📖 Union Terms — Plain English
CBA
Collective Bargaining Agreement — your contract.
Past Practice
A consistent way something is done — enforceable even if not in writing.
Just Cause
Employer must prove a legitimate reason before disciplining you.
Duty of Fair Rep
Your union's legal obligation to represent every member fairly.
Arbitration
Final step of a grievance — neutral third party decides.
MOU
Memo of Understanding — side letter that modifies the CBA.
