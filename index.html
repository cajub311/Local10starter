📜 Weingarten Rights

Your right to union representation during questioning

“If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, I respectfully request that my union representative be present.”

What this means: You have the legal right (NLRB v. Weingarten, 1975) to have a union steward present before answering any questions that could lead to discipline. Say the script, then stay silent until your rep arrives.

☢ Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

You have the legal right to refuse any task you reasonably believe poses an imminent danger of death or serious physical harm.

MN Statute 182.654, Subd. 11 — An employee who refuses in good faith to perform assigned work because of a reasonable belief that it exposes them to an imminent danger of death or serious injury is protected from retaliation.

✔ Tell your supervisor you believe the task is unsafe.

✔ Explain why you believe there's an imminent danger.

✔ Contact your steward and MNOSHA immediately.

💰 MN Wages & Time Laws

🚨 Wage Theft Protection

By MN law, changes to your pay must be in writing BEFORE they happen.

MN Statute 181.101 — Your employer must give you written notice of any pay rate changes before the pay period they take effect.

🩺 Earned Sick & Safe Time (ESST)

MN law requires employers to provide Earned Sick and Safe Time.

You earn 1 hour for every 30 hours worked (up to 48 hrs/year). Use it for illness, safety, or family care. Your employer cannot retaliate for using it.

⏰ Overtime

Time-and-a-half after 40 hours in a workweek — no exceptions.

MN follows federal FLSA. No "comp time" without your written agreement.
