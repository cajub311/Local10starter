🤝 Know Your Rights — In Your Pocket
🛡️
Weingarten Rights — Read If Boss Asks Questions

Before you answer any questions from a supervisor that could lead to discipline, say this word-for-word:

"If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, I respectfully request that my union representative be present at this meeting. Without representation, I choose not to answer any questions."

📖 Union Terms — Plain English
CBA
Collective Bargaining Agreement — your contract.
Past Practice
A consistent way something is done — enforceable even if not in writing.
Just Cause
Employer must prove a legitimate reason before disciplining you.
Duty of Fair Rep
Your union's legal obligation to represent every member fairly.
Arbitration
Final step of a grievance — neutral third party decides.
MOU
Memo of Understanding — side letter that modifies the CBA.
