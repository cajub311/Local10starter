Weingarten Rights — say this out loud
If it could lead to discipline, request your union rep and stop answering questions.
“If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, I respectfully request that my union representative be present. I will not answer questions without my rep.”
Unsafe Work — stop-work steps
If you honestly believe the hazard could kill or seriously injure you, slow down and follow this order.
  1. Say the exact hazard out loud (what, where, and how it can hurt someone).
  2. Ask for a safer method or different task.
  3. Document: photos/video, time, who told you, who witnessed it.
  4. Call your steward immediately.
