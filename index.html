🛡️ Know Your Rights — Floor Protocol

When someone outside your chain of command (office staff, sales, etc.) tries to give you orders or complain about a miscount:

  • Stay calm and professional. You have the right to work without improper interference.
  • Politely redirect: "I report to [Operations Management / my supervisor]. Please take this up with them."
  • Don't accept discipline from anyone outside your chain of command. Only Operations Management (Plant Manager, Production Supervisors) can direct your work and discipline.
  • Document it. Note date, time, who approached you, and what was said. Tell your Steward.

Per the contract: Production employees report to Operations Management. Office/sales staff cannot displace or micromanage union work.

Weingarten Rights

You have the right to union representation during any meeting that could lead to discipline. If you're pulled into the office, you can read this verbatim:

"If this discussion could in any way lead to my being disciplined or terminated, or affect my personal working conditions, I request that my union representative, steward, or coworker be present at this meeting. Until I have this representation, I choose not to answer any questions."

💰 Benefits Quick-Reference (Exhibit B)

Contract legalese simplified. Always refer to the full contract and SPDs for official terms.

ESST (Earned Sick & Safe Time)

  • • Accrual: 1 hour for every 30 hours worked
  • • Max per year: 48 hours
  • • Max carryover: 80 hours into next year
  • • Min increment: 15 minutes
  • • No waiting period — use as you accrue

Wellness Incentive (HSA)

  • • Complete annual physical + USPSTF preventive screenings
  • • Single: Up to $600/year ($11.54/week) into your HSA
  • • Family/other: Up to $1,200/year ($23.08/week) into your HSA
  • • Effective 4/1/25 — provide documentation to HR

Medical Insurance

  • • Single: Company pays 85%
  • • Family/other tiers: Company pays 80%
  • • Spousal surcharge $150 if spouse has access to employer coverage (effective 1/1/26)

Dental Insurance

  • • Cost share: 50% Company, 50% employee
  • • Delta Preferred Option or Select Plus (choice at open enrollment)

Vacation Accrual

  • • Year 1: 40 hrs (0.77/hr per week)
  • • Years 2–5: 80 hrs (1.54/hr per week)
  • • Years 6–10: 120 hrs (2.31/hr per week)
  • • Years 11–19: 160 hrs (3.08/hr per week)
  • • Years 20+: 200 hrs (3.85/hr per week)
