MN Law: Right to Refuse Dangerous Work

Under Minnesota Statute 182.654, workers have protections when refusing tasks that expose them to an imminent danger of death or serious physical harm, especially when there is not enough time to correct the hazard through regular enforcement channels.

Use this exact phrase with your supervisor: "I am requesting hazard correction before continuing this task due to serious safety risk."
