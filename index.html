Unsafe Work

Minnesota workers can refuse dangerous work under MN Statute 182.654 when there is a good-faith belief that performing the task would expose them to death or serious physical harm.

  • Tell your supervisor the specific hazard immediately.
  • Request correction or safer alternative work.
  • Document date, time, location, and witnesses.
  • Contact your steward/union rep right away.
