⚠️ Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

You can refuse work that poses imminent danger to your life or health. This right is protected under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.176.

Sheet Metal Example — Can't force post-fire work until safe: After a shop fire, your employer cannot legally force you to return until the building is certified as safe by the appropriate authority. That is imminent danger. OSHA 29 CFR 1910.176.

For all other unsafe conditions: work under protest, document everything, and file a grievance immediately. See contract Article 6. Your Steward can help you document the refusal correctly.

🌡️ Heat Illness Prevention — Know Your Rights

⚠️ Current Status (Feb 2026)

Federal OSHA proposed a heat rule in 2024 but it has NOT been finalized yet. However, you are STILL protected:

  • OSHA Heat National Emphasis Program (NEP) — Active through April 2026. OSHA is inspecting and citing employers for heat hazards using the General Duty Clause.
  • Minnesota Rule 5205.0110 — MN has its OWN indoor heat standard. Your employer must control indoor heat based on work intensity and temperature.

At 80°F Heat Index

  • Cool drinking water — at least 1 quart/hour per worker
  • Shaded rest areas (outdoor) or fans/AC (indoor)
  • Paid rest breaks as needed
  • New workers: gradual acclimatization

At 90°F Heat Index

  • Everything above PLUS:
  • 15-min paid rest break every 2 hours
  • Buddy system or supervisor monitoring
  • Pre-shift hazard alert

🚨 Warning Signs

Heat Exhaustion

Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache. Action: Move to shade, cool water, rest.

Heat Stroke (EMERGENCY)

No sweating, hot/red skin, confusion, seizures. Action: CALL 911, cool with water/ice.

✊ What You Can Do Right Now

  • Ask for water, shade, and breaks — your employer must provide a safe workplace under the General Duty Clause
  • Report unsafe heat: MN OSHA 651-284-5050 or Federal OSHA 800-321-6742
  • You CANNOT be fired for reporting — retaliation is illegal
  • New to job or returning from 14+ days off? — Highest risk. Demand gradual work increase.
  • Contact your steward if employer refuses water, shade, or rest breaks
