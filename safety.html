Safety Tips & Your Rights

Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

You can refuse work that poses an imminent danger. Example: Post-fire — can't force return until building is deemed safe.

OSHA 29 CFR 1910.176 — Safe material handling. Employer must provide a safe workplace.

Common Hazards

  • PPE for sharp edges: Gloves, cut-resistant sleeves
  • Dust masks: Required when cutting, grinding, welding
  • Eye protection: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.133 — Safety glasses in all shop areas
  • Hearing protection: Paint Shop, Grinding areas, Flat Cell — OSHA 1910.138

Heat Illness Prevention

New OSHA 2026 rule: rest, water, shade required.

Sheet Metal Tips

  • Proper lifting: Bend knees, keep load close
  • Machine guarding: Never remove guards — report damaged guards
  • Ergonomics: Avoid repetitive strain — take breaks, stretch
