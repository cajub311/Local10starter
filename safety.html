🛑 RIGHT TO REFUSE UNSAFE WORK

You have the legal right to refuse work you believe is imminently dangerous.

  1. Tell your supervisor you believe the work is unsafe.
  2. Request correction. If they refuse, you may refuse the task.
  3. Report to your steward and document the incident.
  4. OSHA protects you from retaliation. Retaliation is illegal.

🦺 PPE — PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

Employers must provide required PPE at no cost to you.

  • Hard hats — Required in areas with overhead hazards
  • Safety glasses / face shields — Eye protection for grinding, welding, cutting
  • Hearing protection — When noise exceeds 85 dB
  • Gloves — Cut-resistant, chemical-resistant as needed
  • Respirators — When required by hazard assessment
  • Fall protection — Harness & lanyard at 4 ft+ (6 ft construction)

If PPE is missing or defective, report to your steward. Do not perform the task without proper protection.

🌡️ HEAT RULES — OSHA 2026

New OSHA heat standard (2026) — mandatory protections:

  • Rest breaks — In shade/cool area when heat index is high
  • Water — Potable water readily available
  • Shade — Access to shaded rest area
  • Acclimatization — Gradual increase for new workers
  • Training — Heat illness signs and first aid

Heat illness can be fatal. If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or confused — stop work, get to shade, drink water, tell someone.

🪑 ERGONOMICS & REPETITIVE STRAIN

Report ergonomic issues before they become injuries.

  • Request adjustable workstations, tools, or lifting aids
  • Rotate tasks when possible to reduce repetitive strain
  • Report wrist, back, or shoulder pain early
  • Workers' comp covers ergonomic injuries — document and report
