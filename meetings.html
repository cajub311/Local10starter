⚖️ Your Rights as a Member

🗳️ Paid Voting Time Off
MN law requires employers to give you time to vote. You cannot be docked pay or penalized. See MN Stat. 204C.04.
⚖️ Jury Duty Protection
You cannot be retaliated against or fired for serving on jury duty. Your job and seniority are protected. MN Stat. 593.50.
🤝 Weingarten Rights
You have the right to a union representative present at any investigatory interview that could lead to discipline. Request your Steward before answering questions.
📢 Right to Organize
You have the right to discuss wages, working conditions, and union organizing with coworkers. Employers cannot prohibit this under the NLRA.
