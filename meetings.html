📅 MEETING SCHEDULE

General membership meetings are held regularly at the hall.

  • Check the union bulletin board or smw10.org for dates
  • Meetings typically monthly — call (651) 770-2388 to confirm
  • Your voice matters — attend to vote on contracts, bylaws, and union business

⚖️ MEMBER RIGHTS

  • Voting — Vote on contracts, officers, and union business
  • Jury duty — MN law protects your job; employer cannot retaliate
  • Time off for union activity — Per contract; check with steward
  • Run for office — Members can run for steward, exec board, etc.
  • Attend meetings — General membership meetings are open to all members
