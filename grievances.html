Grievances, FAQs & Concerns

"Work now, grieve later?" — When can I refuse?

You can refuse only if the work is imminently dangerous. Otherwise, do the work and file a grievance. Document everything. See contract Article 6 (Grievance Procedure).

→ Search contract
Overtime disputes — Who gets it?

Overtime is voluntary. Sign up on posted sheets. Based on: workload, machine availability, skillset, seniority. See contract Section 5C.

→ Search contract
Seniority bumping — How does layoff/recall work?

Plant-wide seniority. Displaced employees can bump least senior in their classification or qualified lower classification. See contract Section 6D & 6E.

→ Search contract
Pay errors — What do I do?

Report to Operations Management and your Steward immediately. Document dates, hours, and discrepancies. Wage theft can be reported to MN DLI: 651-284-5075.

→ MN DLI
Harassment — Who do I tell?

Tell your Steward and HR. Document incidents. The contract prohibits harassment. You have the right to a safe workplace.

→ Search contract
Wage theft — How do I report?

MN Dept of Labor: 651-284-5075 or dli.mn.gov. You can file anonymously. Protected from retaliation.

Misclassification as contractor — Am I really an employee?

If you're told when/where to work, use company tools, and can't work for others, you may be misclassified. Contact your Steward and DLI.

Retaliation protection — Can I be fired for filing?

MN law and OSHA protect whistleblowers. Retaliation for reporting safety, wage theft, or filing grievances is illegal. Document and contact your Steward.

Filing steps — What's the grievance procedure?

Step 1: Meeting with aggrieved + Steward + Ops Management (48 hrs). Step 2: Written grievance, meeting with President (10 days). Step 3: FMCS mediation. Step 4: Binding arbitration. See contract Section 6C.

Zero-exposure workers' comp rules

Report all injuries immediately. You have the right to medical care and wage replacement. Light duty may be offered. Contact your Steward. Never sign anything without union rep present.
