Put Local 10 Hub On A Phone

Scan the QR code or tap the download button. This installs the unofficial Local 10 Hub as a standalone Android app with contract search, rights, safety, grievances, meetings, and benefits.

Download APK
  1. Open this page on an Android phone.
  2. Tap Download APK.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from the browser.
  4. Open Local 10 Hub from the app screen.

This is a debug APK for member testing. If a future update refuses to install, uninstall the old debug copy first, then install the newest APK from this same page.

For coworkers: this hub is unofficial and informational. For real deadlines, discipline, injuries, or benefits decisions, call the steward or hall and verify.
