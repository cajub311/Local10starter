# Atlas Manufacturing / SMART Local 10
## Unilateral Change to Material Handler Job Duties – Analysis & Ready-to-Send Notice

**Date of Analysis:** July 22, 2026  
**Issue:** Supervisor revised an old 2016 Material Handler job description, added “backup truck driver” duties, and presented it as the new official description. Supervisor stated she can modify any job description at any time.

---

### Summary of Contractual Violation

The Company does **not** have the right to unilaterally revise job classifications or job duties during the term of the current Collective Bargaining Agreement (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2027).

Both the wage classifications (**Exhibit C**) and the corresponding job duties (**Exhibit D**) are expressly made part of the Agreement and are **binding on both parties** for the full term of the contract.

---

### Exact Contract Language

**Section 7.C – Wages (Page 28 of the contract)**

> “The Company and the Union agree that the schedule setting forth job classification rates marked **Exhibit C** and corresponding job duties marked **Exhibit D**, hereby made a part of this Agreement, **shall be binding upon each party during the term of this Agreement**. The rates set out after each classification shall be the rates to be paid **during the life of this Agreement**. … The Company agrees to make available through ADP job duties, marked as Exhibit D, for corresponding job classifications listed in Exhibit C.”

**Section 1.B – Company Agreements (Page 4)**

> The Company agreed it will “**not enter into any agreement with the employees individually or collectively, which in any way conflicts with the terms or provisions of this Agreement**.”

**Section 4.A – Job Opportunities / Out-of-Class Work (Page 9)**

> Limits continuous assignment of work normally performed in a higher-rated classification beyond two (2) weeks without a compromise involving Operations Management, the Union Steward, and the affected employee. Truck Driver is a separate, higher-paid classification.

---

### Why the Supervisor’s Action Is Incorrect

1. Exhibit D job duties are contractual and cannot be rewritten by management alone.
2. Material Handler and Truck Driver are listed as **separate classifications** in Exhibit C with different wage rates.
3. Taking a 2016 document, revising it, adding duties that belong to another classification, and declaring it “the new one” is a classic unilateral change to a mandatory subject of bargaining.
4. The supervisor’s statement that she can “modify any description on job things” at any time is inconsistent with the clear binding language on Page 28.

---

### Ready-to-Send Message for Business Representative

**Subject:** Unilateral Change to Material Handler Job Description – Request for Guidance

Business Representative,

I need your review on a job classification issue at Atlas.

**What the supervisor did:**  
A supervisor told us that Material Handlers now need to know how to be backup truck drivers. She stated she can modify any job description at any time. She took an old 2016 job description, revised it by adding the backup truck driver duties, and presented the revised version as “the new one.”

**Why this violates the current CBA:**

The Company does **not** have the right to unilaterally revise job duties or classifications during the term of the Agreement.

**Controlling language – Section 7.C (Wages) – Page 28 of the contract:**

> “The Company and the Union agree that the schedule setting forth job classification rates marked **Exhibit C** and corresponding job duties marked **Exhibit D**, hereby made a part of this Agreement, **shall be binding upon each party during the term of this Agreement**. The rates set out after each classification shall be the rates to be paid **during the life of this Agreement**. … The Company agrees to make available through ADP job duties, marked as Exhibit D, for corresponding job classifications listed in Exhibit C.”

This language makes both the classifications (Exhibit C) **and** the job duties (Exhibit D) contractual and binding through March 31, 2027. The Company cannot rewrite them on its own.

**Additional supporting language:**

- **Section 1.B (Company Agreements) – Page 4:**  
  The Company agreed it will “**not enter into any agreement with the employees individually or collectively, which in any way conflicts with the terms or provisions of this Agreement**.”

- **Section 4.A (Job Opportunities) – Page 9:**  
  Limits continuous assignment of higher-classification work (Truck Driver is a separate, higher-paid classification) beyond two weeks without agreement involving the Steward and the employee.

**Bottom line:**  
Revising an old 2016 job description, adding new duties that belong to a different classification, and calling it “the new one” is a unilateral change to the bargained-for job content. That action is inconsistent with the clear language on **Page 28** (Section 7.C).

Please advise on next steps. I can provide the revised description the supervisor issued. I am prepared to move forward under the grievance procedure if needed.

Thank you.
