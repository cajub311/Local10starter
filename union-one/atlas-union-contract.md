## 1. General Matters/Activities

This is an Agreement, made and entered into by and between Atlas Manufacturing, a Division of Meribel Enterprises, LLC located at 2950 Weeks Avenue S. E. Minneapolis, MN and the International Association of Sheet Metal, Air, Rail and Transportation Workers Local 10. By mutual consent of both parties, this Agreement establishes rules and regulations to govern employment, wage scales and working conditions of production employees and non-office indirect labor floor employees as established and recognized under this Agreement.

For clarification of agreement language:
- **Company** = Atlas Manufacturing, a Division of Meribel Enterprises, LLC
- **Union** = International Association of Sheet Metal, Air, Rail and Transportation Workers Local 10
- **Operations Management** = Plant Manager or Production Supervisors

### A. Company and Union Agreements

The Company and Union agree that, as of the signing of this contract the following actions will be deemed complete:

- Any employee hired prior to 1 November 2002, will have a seniority date of 1 November 2002.
- Employees hired after 1 November 2002, will have seniority date as date of hire.
- The Company will recognize employee original hire date for purpose of years of service awards and vacation accrual. Employees of Atlas Manufacturing prior to Meribel Enterprises acquisition on September 26, 2002, have a Grandfather clause and will maintain existing personal and vacation time balances.
- All Employees' attendance and disciplinary records will start, as of September 26, 2002.
- Employees will start with a clean slate with no attendance or individual disciplinary records, as of 1 November 2002.
- The Company will retain all original new hire paperwork, work qualification, training and safety letters/records.
- The Company and Union agree that during the term of this Agreement there shall be no strikes, stoppage or slowdowns of work or lockouts.

### B. Company Agreements

- Not enter into any agreement with the employees individually or collectively, which in any way conflicts with the terms or provisions of this Agreement
- Not discriminate in any manner against the employees because of membership or activity in the Union.
- Provide access to the plant for an authorized representative of the Union (Business Agent) during normal shift hours when the plant is open when prior notice is given to the Company's Operations Management.
- Provide access to the plant for an authorized representative of the Union (Business Agent) during times when the plant is closed by arrangement with the Company's Operations Management.
- To ensure that employee is offered Union representation when called before management to be reprimanded. Union employee has right to sign away representation with waiver pending Company approval. Exclusion to this requirement is verbal non-recorded counseling that doesn't result in formal reprimand.
- Security cameras will be used only for the purpose of protecting the property of the Company and employees against criminal acts such as theft, depredation, damage to property, the monitoring of equipment and safety for all employees. At no time will the camera system be used as a means to evaluate an employee's performance or to gather evidence in support of disciplinary actions, unless such disciplinary actions result from a commission of a criminal act or injury. Recording devices will not be used in non-work areas in which employees have an expectation of privacy, such as bathrooms, changing rooms, areas designated for expressing breast milk. The Company may from time to time install and use additional video recording devices in the plant. The Company will notify the Union of the location of any additional cameras in the plant. The Company will retain video information for a maximum of 31 days.
- Ensure Part-time, Temporary, and Intermittent employees do not exceed 10% of overall workforce.

### C. Union Agreements

- Not to carry on any union activities during working hours with the following exceptions:
  - Settlement of grievances provided that the handling of such grievances is done in a non-disruptive manner
  - Union benefits informational meetings
  - Union committee and Company management meetings
- Coordinate with Company's Operations Management to ensure expeditious accomplishment and coordination for all activities so as not to jeopardize or disrupt operations.
- Provide a duly elected Union Committee that shall represent employees individually and collectively in all collective bargaining with the Company, and other relative matters as may be delegated to it. This Committee shall consist of (1) designated Union Steward and (4) committee members selected from all shifts to provide the best overall democratic representations possible based on the number of employees on each shift. Meeting and payment of this Committee will be negotiated and coordinated with the Union Business Agent and the Company's Operations Management.

## 2. Recognition and Employment Status Definitions

### A. Union

All regular full-time and part-time production employees and non-office indirect labor floor employees as indicated by position, job description and wage classification rates in Exhibits, of this agreement. Note: Any regular full-time and part-time production employee or non-office indirect labor floor employee can perform of voluntary free will, as offered by Company in limited non-negotiated form, work in office indirect or technical positions. The only limiting factor will be that they cannot perform any supervisory functions outside of normal lead duties, as not to create conflict with other Union workers.

### B. Company

All regular full-time office employees, both exempt and non-exempt pay status, who work in professional, supervisory, administrative, technical or sales positions.

These employees will not perform production work with intentions to displace or cause loss of normal regular work for a Union employee covered in this agreement. These employees can perform production work when coordinated by the Operations Management and/or communicated to the Union Steward and a Business Representative of the Union, in advance, for the following reasons:

- To maintain or gain on floor work proficiency or experience
- To gain firsthand knowledge and provide efficiency when prototyping new parts maintaining cost competitiveness and customer satisfaction in non-production part runs
- To assist or fill in Union labor shortage due to excessive vacation, personal time or excessive absence of normal Union production labor
- When unexpected work increase has caused labor shortage, only after initiation of expeditious efforts to hire personnel to replace required labor on part time or full-time basis.

All temporary or intermittent employees, who work in professional, supervisory, administrative, technical, sales positions and limited production workers when unexpected work increase has caused immediate short term labor shortage.

Any employment of production employees and non-office indirect labor floor employees as indicated by position, job description and wage classification rates in Exhibits, of this agreement, will be offered and controlled by the following requirements and actions:

- There will be no part-time, temporary, or intermittent production employees hired during a layoff unless deemed appropriate through decline of work by layoff workers, as verified by Union Steward and a Business Representative of the Union
- Unexpected work increases must be noted and reported to Union Steward and a Business Representative of the Union prior to hiring of part-time, temporary, or intermittent employees.
- Any hiring of part-time, temporary, or intermittent employee must meet the following criteria:
  - Part-time, Temporary or Intermittent employees may not exceed 30 hours per week
  - These employees will not perform production work with intentions to displace or cause loss of normal regular work for a Union employee covered in this agreement.
  - These employees will work primarily in non-technical positions with limited utilization of major equipment, to limit safety and work comp risk.
  - Primary positions will be Sheet Metal Fabricator I or no higher than the lowest level in any other job classification, major exceptions will be when integrated with Union worker.
  - As of 4/1/23, existing Part-time, Temporary, or Intermittent employees will be allowed to remain in their current classification.
  - Any Full-time employee with a minimum of ten (10) years seniority will be allowed to work part time in any job classification level.
  - Any Part-time, Temporary, or Intermittent employee will not displace or deny the rights of a full time Union employee to work overtime.
  - Any Part-time, Temporary, or Intermittent employees are not eligible for Company benefits.
  - Should any non-union employee work on the floor as provided for in Article 2, union members capable of working in that specific resource will be allowed to work unlimited overtime in accordance with all other sections of the CBA.

## 3. Union Security and Dues Deduction

All employees covered by this Agreement shall be required as a condition of employment to become and remain members of the Union in good standing during the term of this Agreement. The test of good standing shall be in accordance with applicable law.

There shall be no discrimination because of Union affiliation. The parties agree that all present employees covered by this Agreement, who are members of the Union in good standing on the effective date of this Agreement shall remain members of the Union in good standing. It shall also be a condition of employment that all employees covered by this Agreement and hired on or after its effective date shall on the completion of the probationary period following the beginning of such employment become and remain members in good standing in the Union.

The Employer agrees upon written authorization from the individual employee, to deduct from each paycheck an equal portion of pay, as collection and payment for the following month's union dues, initiation fees and such other deductions as may now or hereafter be permissible under the Taft-Hartley Act, as amended. The Company will monthly remit same to the Financial Secretary of the Union, with an itemized statement showing from whom deducted and the amounts. The Company further agrees that shall any employee have no earnings due during any week, deductions shall be made from the next succeeding pay of the employee.

Any employee covered by this Agreement who voluntarily refuses to go through a picket line on the premises of this Employer, or any other premises where a labor dispute exists, shall not be deemed to have violated this Agreement.

## 4. Job Opportunities, Probation & Orientation Periods

### A. Job Opportunities

Job opportunities are Company determined based on work requirements and organizational needs. As the Company grows or requirements change, there will be a requirement for the changing of the work force. To facilitate these needs of upward change, in a fair and equitable manner, the following procedures and actions need to occur:

**1. Company:**
- When possible, encourage cross training, trial and training in higher classifications of employees to enable upward advancement.
- Limit assigning of work normally performed in a job classification with a higher rate of pay except for purpose of training or cross training. If an event occurs that would require more than (2) weeks of continuous work in a class of higher pay – Operations Management, Union Steward and the individual affected will come to a compromise or remedy for the situation. If none can be reached, the individual is not required to work upward out of class.
- Identify job opportunities
- Ensure posting is for the position and/or class that meets the Company's labor requirements. (Company does not have to post lesser classifications, i.e., "trainee")
- Post opportunities in the form of a "Job Posting" (Job Posting Form, Exhibit A, this Agreement), to be displayed for (3) working days on the Company bulletin board.
- Operations Management will do the following:
  - Develop interview criteria and questions based on, but not limited to, seniority, attendance, specific job skills, technical knowledge, communication, learning aptitude, quality and individual job performance efficiencies
  - Interview posting candidates based on established questions and interview criteria.
  - Rank individuals, determine qualifications of posted individuals, recommend selection or non-selection and compile list of qualified interviewees, for new position selection, which will be valid for (3) months.
  - Provide feedback to interviewees.
  - Ensure that all interviewed and qualified employees will have an opportunity for advancement prior to hiring of outside personal.
  - Be prepared to conduct additional interviews, ranking, determine qualifications, recommend selection or non-selection of personnel outside the organization, based on non-qualification of existing personnel.
  - Notify the Union Steward of the final selection.
  - Selected employee will not be allowed to post for another position for a period of 6 months.

**2. Union Employees:**
- Perform current position to high standard and prepare for the future.
- Seek and actively strive for advancement, in active choice to work upward or downward out of class for training and cross training purposes.
- Sign for postings that you feel you are qualified.
- Conduct yourself to the best of your ability during interview process
- Excel in work in new position to prove worth of selection (both during probation as well as everyday thereafter)

### B. Probation & Orientation Periods

| Probation Type | Length | Seniority | Union Dues |
|----------------|--------|-----------|------------|
| New Hire | (3) Months | Seniority = Date of hire | Start paying upon completion |
| Temp/Hire | (3) + (1) Months | Seniority = Date of hire | Start paying upon completion |
| New Position | (1) Month | No Change | No change |
| Part Time/Intermittent | 480 hours | Seniority = Date of hire | Start paying upon completion |

During the probationary period, the Company shall have the right to retain in employment (new or temporary employee) or position (current employees), or the right to discharge (new or temporary employees only) or return to old position (current employees). The probationary employee may not challenge his/her discharge or return to old position through the grievance procedure.

## 5. Hours/Rest Periods/Overtime/Attendance/Absences

### A. Core Shift Hours of Operation

| Shift | Hours | Days |
|-------|-------|------|
| 1st shift | 6:00 a.m. – 2:30 p.m. | Monday – Friday |
| 2nd shift | 2:30 p.m. – 1:00 a.m. | Monday – Thursday |
| 3rd shift | 6:00 a.m. – 6:30 p.m. | Friday – Sunday |
| Flex Shift | 5:00 a.m. – 1:00 a.m. | Monday – Friday |

No employee will work more than 12 hours per shift. All employees will have an 8-hour non-pay break between shifts.

Whenever an employee who has finished his/her regularly scheduled shift and left the Company premises is called back by the Company to work, said employee shall be paid portal to portal including mileage at the published IRS rate.

Pay during voluntary travel for company business shall be at the core hourly rate and up to a maximum of eight (8) hours. Overtime pay will only apply if the travel extends beyond the normal scheduled work week for that employee.

Pay for training will be paid at the core hourly rate regardless of the hours worked for the day or week and regardless of the day worked.

### B. Rest Periods

**Break periods** (paid): Employees will individually select their break periods between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 10 a.m. or between 5:00 p.m. and 6:30 p.m. and between 9:30 p.m. and 11:00 p.m. Employees may NOT leave the premises during a paid break.

| Hours Worked Per Day | Number of Breaks | Break Length |
|---------------------|------------------|--------------|
| Fewer than 4 | 0 | No Break |
| 4 but fewer than 7 | 1 | 10 minutes |
| 7 but fewer than 10 | 2 | 10 minutes |
| 10 or more | 2 | 1 – 10 minute, 1 – 15 minute |

**Lunch periods** (non-paid): 30 minutes, selected individually between 11:00 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. or between 7:00 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. Lunch periods may not be combined with break periods. Employees must clock out and back in if they leave the premises for their lunch period.

### C. Overtime

| Pay | Conditions |
|-----|------------|
| 1½ | Any time worked beyond normal length of shift (core shift hours must be worked) |
| 1½ | Any time worked beyond normal length of week |
| 2× | Any hours worked on the 7th day or hours worked in excess of 60 |
| 3× | Holiday, normal holiday pay plus 2 times hours worked (must be individually approved by Operations Management) |

For purposes of computing overtime, the following shall be counted as time worked for a full day (full day = 8 hours): paid holidays, Vacation, jury duty and bereavement leave.

### D. Premium Pay

Employees working the 3rd shift will receive 4 hours premium pay as long as the core shift hours have been worked.

### E. Attendance (No-Fault Point System)

- **New Employee** – Start at zero (0) points upon hire
- **Time Period** – Calendar year January 1st – December 31st (pro-rated for new hires). Points return to zero (0) every January 1st
- **Point-free absences:** Vacation, Reserve duty, Holidays, Contractual time off, Jury duty, Industrial injury (Work Comp), Approved LOA, Company excused due to lack of work, Bereavement, Emergency situations, ESST
- **Points assessed:** All other absences = one (1) point per day missed
- **Absences Without Proper Notification:** Report no later than one (1) hour after start of shift. If not reported within first hour: ½ point. Failure to report during scheduled shift: two (2) points
- **Corrective Action:** 2 points = verbal warning; 4 points = written warning; 5 points = second written warning + 2-day unpaid suspension; 6 points = subject to termination

### F. Absences

**Personal Time:** May be used in increments as small as 1 hour. Must request in ADP at least one (1) hour prior to taking and receive documented acknowledgement.

**Vacation:** Accrual schedule:
- Year 1: 40 hours (0.77 hrs/week)
- Years 2-5: 80 hours (1.54 hrs/week)
- Years 6-10: 120 hours (2.31 hrs/week)
- Years 11-19: 160 hours (3.08 hrs/week)
- Years 20+: 200 hours (3.85 hrs/week)

**ESST (Earned Sick and Safe Time):** 1 hour for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours per benefit year. Max carryover: 80 hours.

**Holidays:** New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve Day, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve Day, Floating Holiday.

**Leave of Absence:** FMLA (up to 12 weeks or 26 weeks for covered servicemember care), Jury Duty, Bereavement (3 days), Personal Leave (up to 6 weeks).

## 6. Seniority/Cause and Discharge/Grievance Procedure/LOW & Layoff/Recall

### A. Seniority

Seniority shall be on a plant-wide basis. An up-to-date seniority list shall be provided monthly to the Union Steward and Business Representative. Any employee who has been laid off for a six (6) month continuous period shall lose all seniority.

### B. Cause and Discharge

The Company shall discharge and discipline the employee for only just cause. An employee must notify the Company in writing within five (5) business days of their intent of a challenge to the discharge.

### C. Grievance Procedure

- **Step 1:** Meeting between aggrieved employee and/or Union Steward and Operations Management. If not resolved within 48 hours, grievance must be submitted in writing within 3 days.
- **Step 2:** Meeting between Union Steward, Business Representative and President or representative(s). If not resolved within 10 working days, proceed to Step 3.
- **Step 3:** Mediation through FMCS within 30 calendar days of notice to arbitrate.
- **Step 4:** Binding arbitration. Arbitrator selected from list of 5 from State Mediation and Conciliation Services. Each party bears own expense; arbitrator expense split equally.

### D. LOW/Layoff

Part-time, temporary, and intermittent employees released first. Company shall notify affected employees, Union Steward and Business Representative at least (3) working days in advance of layoff. Employees who report for work without prior notice shall be guaranteed two (2) hours of work.

### E. Recall

Employees recalled in order of seniority. Must have skills and qualifications to perform the work satisfactorily.

## 7. Health & Welfare/Uniforms/Wages/Pension Plan

### A. Health & Welfare Benefits

- Health insurance (cost shared: 85% Company for single, 80% for other tiers)
- Dental insurance (50% Company, 50% employee)
- Term Life and AD&D (employee paid)
- Long Term Disability, Short Term Disability (employee paid)
- Flex Dependent Care, Flex Medical Reimbursement, HSA (employee paid)
- Voluntary Accident, Critical Illness, Hospital Indemnity, Vision (employee paid)

**Effective 1/1/26:** $150 spousal surcharge if spouse has access to employer coverage.

**Wellness incentive (4/1/25):** Up to $600/year single, $1,200/year other coverages for HSA contribution when completing annual physical and USPSTF preventive screenings.

### B. Uniforms

Company may make uniform apparel available through outside service. Participation requires authorization for wage deduction.

### C. Wages

Shift differentials:
- 1st shift: $0.00
- 2nd shift: $1.25 (effective 4/1/23)
- 3rd shift: $1.00 + 4 hours Premium Pay (for 36 core hours)

See Exhibit C for full wage scale.

### D. Pension Plan

- Effective 4/1/2024: 4.5% of Gross Wages to Sheet Metal Workers Union Local 10 Pension Fund
- Payments due 1st of month, payable by 15th
- Voluntary contributions and 401(k) available

## 8. Shop Rules/Health & Safety

### A. Shop Rules

- Bulletin boards for Union use
- Proper use of Company property
- Purchase of equipment/materials: payroll deduction available for employees 1+ year, position-required purchases over $250

### B. Health & Safety
